Behind The Numbers

What Each Number Means

Clean Actions = submitted applications + valid standalone outreach. Follow-ups, invalid routes and application-linked emails do not add another opportunity.

Active Applications are confirmed submissions without a later rejection, closure or withdrawal. Interview-stage applications remain active. Outreach is separate.

Interview Conversion = distinct processes reaching a human interview or callback ÷ submitted applications. It does not estimate the chance of getting a job.

Forecast extends the average daily clean-action pace from 1 August to the last audit. It is an illustrative scenario, not verified activity after that audit. A historical line appears only when complete dated evidence is published.
