One Consistent Starting Point

The reporting window and every widget share one committed source watermark. Dates group in Europe/London. With no connected source, counts are unavailable, not factual zeroes.

What Is Counted

Unique Approaches preserves the legacy minimum-evidence concept: distinct evidenced company + role processes plus unmatched outreach targets. An attempt is not proof of delivery.

Application Processes counts exact-role submission receipts. Prepared packs, opened forms and unknown outcomes do not count as verified submissions. A failed outreach send never removes an application receipt.

Forecast extends the observed pace as a labelled illustration only. It is unavailable without complete source data.
